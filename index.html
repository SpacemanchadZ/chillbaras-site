FAQ

Which network?

Ethereum mainnet. You'll need ETH in your wallet for gas. Every Chonk Bot lives on Ethereum forever.

How are tiers decided?

Each trait's rarity adds to a score. The 10 one-of-ones are Legendary. Then the top 3% are Epic, the next 10% Rare, the next 25% Uncommon, and the rest Common.

Can the art change after mint?

No. Once mint is over, the metadata is locked on-chain forever.

Royalties?

5% on secondary sales, which funds the community and future drops.
