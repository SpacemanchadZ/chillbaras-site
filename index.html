FAQ

What happens when I wake a raccoon up?

Your Tired raccoon is burned (destroyed for good) and you get a random raccoon from the Awake pool in the same transaction. Every Awake raccoon is unique, and once it's claimed it's gone from the pool.

Do I have to wake mine up?

No. Tired raccoons are a full collection on their own. Every wake-up makes Tired ones scarcer.

Which network?

Ethereum mainnet. You'll need ETH for gas.

How are tiers decided?

Rarity is scored within each phase. The 1/1s are Legendary, then the top 3% are Epic, the next 10% Rare, the next 25% Uncommon, and the rest Common.

Can the art change after mint?

The contract has a one-way metadata lock. Once it's used, the art and traits can never be changed.

Royalties?

5% on secondary sales.
